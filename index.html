Build a book from your idea

Tell the AI your story, movie idea or concept. It will create an original book plan for you.

You can mention a film, series or book as inspiration. Chapter One will use only broad genre and mood ideas, then create a new story with original characters, setting and plot.

PDF, DOCX, TXT and MD are supported. Scanned PDFs containing only images need OCR and cannot be extracted in this version.
